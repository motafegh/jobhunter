# Roadmap Amendment — Utility, Reasoning, and Promotion in Phase 2+

**Status:** CONTROLLING ROADMAP AMENDMENT  
**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Amends:** `docs/ROADMAP.md` for Phase 2 and later analytical delivery  
**Governed by:** `docs/UTILITY_EPISTEMIC_AUTHORITY_AND_REASONING_POLICY.md`

---

## 1. Decision

The strategic sequence remains:

```text
P2.1 canonical registry — CLOSED
→ P2.2 responsibilities/deliverables/role intelligence
→ P2.3 job capability requirement profiles
→ P2.4 Market v2
→ Phase 3 reviewed personal evidence
→ later gap/action/application intelligence
```

What changes is **how useful intelligence may appear before canonical promotion**.

Phase 2 must no longer be interpreted as:

```text
canonicalize/review everything
→ then reason
→ then show useful intelligence
```

The intended strategy is:

```text
trustworthy factual substrate
→ fast transparent interpretation
→ candidate reusable semantics
→ selective review/promotion proportional to reuse/blast radius
→ canonical aggregation where appropriate
```

---

## 2. Phase-2 product objective

Stage R1 remains a canonical semantic market model, but its user-facing objective is now explicit:

> Help the user understand what jobs actually involve, how they differ, what patterns appear, and what likely role/capability structure exists **without requiring exhaustive manual canonicalization first**.

The canonical layer is infrastructure for stable reuse and aggregation. It is not a prerequisite for every useful job-level conclusion.

---

## 3. P2.2 responsibilities, deliverables, and role intelligence

P2.2 should build two related outputs.

### 3.1 Fast job-level role/work intelligence

May be generated from accepted/current factual substrate and clearly labeled semantic reasoning.

Examples:

- work-composition summary;
- key responsibility clusters;
- likely deliverables;
- candidate responsibility-family assignments;
- candidate role/archetype interpretation;
- title-versus-work observations;
- important ambiguity/unknowns.

These outputs do not need prior promotion into canonical families/archetypes to be useful.

### 3.2 Promoted reusable role semantics

Stronger review applies to reusable concepts such as:

- canonical responsibilities;
- responsibility families;
- accepted deliverable concepts;
- stable role archetypes;
- durable cross-job family memberships.

Promotion should be selective and driven by actual downstream reuse/aggregation needs.

P2.2 must not become a program to manually normalize every responsibility in the accepted corpus before job-level intelligence can exist.

---

## 4. Role archetype strategy

Preserve the evidence-derived rule: do not force jobs into a predefined title taxonomy.

Refine it as follows:

```text
candidate archetype
→ may be produced from one/few jobs as an explicitly tentative interpretation

promoted stable archetype
→ requires stronger cross-job/employer evidence, definition, boundaries, and review
```

Small samples can support hypotheses and useful comparisons. They cannot support unqualified market prevalence or universal role-taxonomy claims.

---

## 5. P2.3 capability requirement profiles

P2.3 should likewise distinguish:

```text
job-specific analytical expectation
→ can be inferred when source/work evidence supports it

promoted cross-job capability relationship
→ requires stronger reviewed reuse evidence
```

A job-specific profile may say a work activity is `strongly_implied_by_work` without first converting that relation into universal taxonomy truth.

Unknown scope remains valid, but uncertainty should reduce confidence/detail rather than erase all useful interpretation.

---

## 6. P2.4 Market v2

Market v2 remains downstream of promoted canonical mappings where canonical counts are claimed.

However, exploratory analytical views may exist before full promotion if they are clearly presented as exploratory/candidate and preserve sample/scope/uncertainty.

Do not conflate:

```text
exploratory pattern
stable canonical market statistic
```

Stable Market v2 aggregates still require the promoted subset necessary for the metric being reported.

---

## 7. Corpus expansion strategy

The accepted semantic corpus should expand because it increases product usefulness and diversity, not because a fixed quota is required for every feature.

Select additional jobs when they answer a concrete need such as:

- repeated responsibility patterns;
- new work patterns;
- similar titles with different work;
- different titles with similar work;
- role-family boundary cases;
- deliverable diversity;
- cross-employer support for a candidate archetype;
- stronger support for a planned aggregate.

Do not require an arbitrary corpus size before job-level intelligence can be shown.

---

## 8. Strategic anti-patterns

Avoid:

- treating all semantic output as if it must become canonical truth;
- blocking a job-level insight because market-level evidence is insufficient;
- using deterministic matching to replace semantic interpretation;
- requiring human review of every generated summary/classification;
- building a fixed role taxonomy first and forcing jobs into it;
- turning every ambiguity into an `unresolved` feature stop;
- performing exhaustive mapping without a downstream decision/query that needs it;
- optimizing for annotation completeness instead of user time saved.

---

## 9. Updated Phase-2 backbone

```text
P2.1 reviewed canonical registry foundation
        ↓
P2.2 fast responsibility/work intelligence
        + selective promoted responsibility/family/archetype knowledge
        ↓
P2.3 job capability requirement intelligence
        + selective promoted capability relations
        ↓
P2.4 canonical Market v2 aggregates
        + clearly separated exploratory analysis where useful
```

The product should become progressively more useful throughout the phase; usefulness must not wait until the end of canonicalization.
