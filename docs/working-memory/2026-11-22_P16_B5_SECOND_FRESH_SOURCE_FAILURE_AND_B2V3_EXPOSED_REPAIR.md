## 3. Retrospective generic B2v3 successor — development only

After the B2v2 failure, a **separate non-production B2v3** candidate was implemented in `src/jobhunter/source_item_candidate_b2_v3.py`. The previous B2/v2 source code, historical evidence and accepted v30/v9 are untouched. B2v3 is intentionally a general bilingual document-structure extension:

- Detects complete source section labels with or without punctuation, including flattened Persian work responsibilities, required vs preferred vs important skills, behavioral expectations, arrangements and native-English responsibilities/requirements/preferences.
- Preserves source-owned exact original character/UTF-8 offsets, SHA identities and B1 candidate-only semantics. Never treats a field/source anchor as a proof of mandatory prior paid employment.
- Distinguishes a technical action such as “analyze requirements” from a separate “requirements” heading. Limits splitting inside governed noun phrases and goal clauses (e.g. threat-log analysis *to identify* risky behavior). Recognizes source-explicit `one or more`/`or equivalent`/`and/or` only as **unreviewed unresolved option scope**, not as a generated proven Boolean applicant graph.
- Keeps old compound Middle/Senior and certificate-equivalence source tracks contiguous, retaining the two previously verified B0 eligibility graphs. Preserves original 4 exposed B0 job duty counts (6, 11, 5, 0). No employer- or posting-ID-specific rules added.
- Handles `is a plus` technical familiarity as preferred; does not impose all listed example tools as prerequisites.
- Leaves intact existing B2v1/B2v2, source-bound B3, B4v5 presentation, v30/v9, Capability/Work/Registry/Market, production database and public web/CLI behavior.

## 5. Engineering judgment and next action

The repeated source-gate failures show that deterministic heading heuristics alone are insufficient for varied employer formatting: more headings improve exposed matches but can accidentally fragment legitimate duties and alternative eligibility. **Stop automatically accumulating heading exceptions**. Preserve B2v3 as a frozen opt-in source-structure candidate, and reassess whether a bounded local model-assisted section interpretation with original source anchors and cautious semantic uncertainty better serves real useful job understanding than ever-growing regex micro-rules. Keep the stronger SHA/offset/state checks as infrastructure; use model reasoning for meaning rather than encoding all employer writing patterns in code.

Before any further B5 attempt, freeze the next candidate/design and source-first assessment protocol, choose a **new distinct unexposed cohort** (the first two are consumed), and decide the separately bounded translation and paired model-call budgets. If a critical unsupported employer requirement, wrong OR/AND, invented duty or translation modality reversal recurs, stop promotion honestly. Useful per-job interpretations may still be shown as clearly labeled candidates with uncertainty, but accepted shared facts and B6 production remain blocked pending stronger evidence.
