# Proposal Refinement — Fast Interpretation, Promotion, and Role Intelligence

## 1. Why this refinement exists

The original semantic-role proposal family correctly protects against false taxonomy and unsupported market claims, but its promotion language can be misread as requiring canonical acceptance before JobHunter may provide useful job-level interpretation.

This refinement makes the intended distinction explicit:

```text
interpret first when useful and traceable
promote selectively when stable reuse needs stronger authority
```

---

## 2. Responsibility intelligence refinement

For B015/B016:

```text
source responsibility
→ candidate semantic normalization / family interpretation
→ useful job-level work composition
→ selective reviewed canonical responsibility/family promotion
```

Candidate family assignment may be one-to-many and confidence-qualified.

A responsibility does not need a promoted family before JobHunter can explain what kind of work it appears to represent.

Promotion remains necessary before a family becomes reusable canonical market structure.

---

## 3. Deliverable intelligence refinement

For B014:

Deliverables may be presented at three levels:

1. source-explicit deliverable;
2. strongly work-implied candidate deliverable;
3. reviewed canonical deliverable.

Do not infer a deliverable merely from generic tool knowledge. But do not require canonical deliverable promotion before showing a clearly labeled work-implied output when the responsibility itself supports it.

---

## 4. Role DNA refinement

For B017:

Role DNA should be considered a **job-level analytical view first**, not a canonical taxonomy object.

It may summarize:

- dominant work areas;
- supporting work areas;
- responsibility clusters;
- deliverables;
- capability emphasis;
- uncertainty/ambiguity.

Exact percentages should still be avoided without a defensible measurement rule.

A Role DNA view can be useful for a single job even when no stable market archetype has been promoted.

---

## 5. Title mismatch refinement

For B018:

A title-versus-work mismatch can be shown as an analytical observation when:

- the underlying responsibility/work evidence is strong;
- JobHunter labels the conclusion as its interpretation;
- it does not claim the employer's title is incorrect.

It does not need to wait for a globally mature archetype taxonomy if the comparison is bounded and explainable.

---

## 6. Role archetype refinement

For B019, introduce two states.

### Candidate archetype

May be generated from limited evidence and shown as a hypothesis.

Required qualities:

- evidence-backed work composition;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- representative job links;
- no market-prevalence claim unless separately supported.

### Promoted archetype

Reusable canonical role pattern.

Requires stronger evidence:

- repeated cross-job pattern;
- preferably cross-employer support where the intended claim needs it;
- explicit definition/boundaries;
- representative examples;
- review/promotion decision;
- ability to preserve hybrid/unmapped jobs.

Do not equate one model cluster with a promoted archetype.
Do not prevent candidate archetype output merely because promotion evidence is not yet sufficient.

---

## 7. Capability relationship refinement

For B024-B028:

Distinguish:

```text
job-specific analytical relation
candidate cross-job relation
reviewed/promoted reusable relation
market co-occurrence statistic
technical prerequisite relation
```

A job-specific work implication may be useful without establishing a universal prerequisite or taxonomy edge.

---

## 8. Sample-size refinement

Use the sample size appropriate to the claim:

- one job can support a job-level work interpretation;
- a few jobs can support a candidate recurring pattern;
- promoted stable archetypes/families require stronger repeated evidence;
- market prevalence requires explicit posting/employer/sample scope.

Avoid arbitrary quotas when they do not correspond to the claim being made.

---

## 9. Review refinement

Human review should focus on semantic decisions that become durable reusable authority.

Do not require the user to manually approve every:

- generated work summary;
- candidate family suggestion;
- candidate archetype;
- exploratory comparison;
- low-blast-radius inferred relation.

Instead, keep those transparent and revisable.

---

## 10. Updated Phase-2 backbone

```text
accepted factual substrate
→ fast job-level work/role intelligence
→ candidate semantic structure
→ selective reviewed promotion
→ stable canonical aggregation
→ later personal comparison
```

This refinement preserves the original guardrails while making user-time savings and analytical usefulness first-class.
