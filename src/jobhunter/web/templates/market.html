Interpretation boundary

What these numbers mean

Every contributing P1.6 artifact has an explicit accepted semantic-review state. Acceptance certifies the reviewed per-posting factual substrate; it does not certify that this bounded sample represents the wider market.

This is the first market layer, not the final Phase 2 taxonomy. Similar concepts such as “PostgreSQL” and “Postgres” may still appear separately until canonicalization is built and reviewed.

Do not interpret percentages or absence from a small analyzed sample as full-market truth. Sample size, corpus coverage, employer concentration, filter scope, and duplicate-adjustment limits are displayed deliberately.
