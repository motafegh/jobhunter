Preset:
How these limits work together

Search terms controls breadth. Request limit is the network safety brake. New jobs and old jobs control the more expensive full detail-page checks.

JobHunter never fetches more than 50 total detail pages in one sync, so “New jobs to fully fetch” + “Old jobs to recheck” must stay at or below 50.

The defaults are intentionally conservative. Increasing the numbers does not improve data quality by itself—it mainly increases coverage, runtime, stored evidence, and requests to Jobinja.
